# Firmware feature board

This board tracks replacement-firmware integration, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Status records evidence from the research unit only; it is not a compatibility guarantee for another unit. For the installed artifact, bench state, evidence index and next task, start with [RESUME.md](RESUME.md).

This board covers what the SDK and hardware can do safely: peripherals, supervision, storage, transport, OTA and the feeding guarantees. Owner-visible behavior built on them (schedules, alerts, lock, light and sound policy, Home Assistant) and stock parity are on [FEATURE_BOARD.md](FEATURE_BOARD.md).

## Run rules (owner, October 26: "finish the firmware board")

- A coordinator agent runs the board: one implementer agent per card in its own worktree, and one reviewer agent per card resumed until it reports nothing blocking (three rounds at most, then the card parks on its branch for the owner). Then the card is rebased, `make check` passes on the result, and it is committed to `master` and pushed.
- Bench: full access, one holder at a time. Unattended motor motion is allowed with the hopper empty, the D cells out (relay 1 is the kill switch) and at most 3 portions a feed. Bank 0 is written only through `wellfed-flash`'s reviewed UART path, and a storage format change ships a bank-0 refresh before a build that writes it. A bounded foreground NTP server on the bench host is allowed for FW46; firewall and system services stay unchanged.
- The logic analyzer is at the motor (D2, D3); every capture records all eight channels.
- Anything that needs the owner (probing, food, button presses, LED observation, batteries) is recorded for them and the card closes at its software scope or stays in progress.

## Using the board

Move cards through **Planned → Ready → In progress → Bench check → Done**. Ready requires completed dependencies. A Done card that leaves part of its finish line to another card says so in its status and in the carried-items table. The Evidence column records what acceptance rests on: physical bench observation, static analysis, or host simulation. Update the board when meaningful implementation or acceptance evidence changes. Keep one integration card in progress; independent software preparation can proceed in parallel.

Hardware acceptance belongs to an explicitly selected bench device and an operator-supervised procedure. No status here authorizes flashing, motion, or another stock round trip. Preserve raw evidence privately and record redacted outcomes. Host tests alone do not complete hardware acceptance.

Prioritize the UART development loop and build identity, stopped startup, LED diagnostics, then real flash coordination and Wi-Fi/OTA. Sensors and durable storage lead into supervised feeding; everyday features and fault/sustained testing follow. See [architecture](docs/firmware/README.md), [startup/indicator diagnostics](docs/firmware/startup-diagnostics.md), [development updater](docs/development/uart-development.md), and [recovery limits](docs/devices/plaf103/recovery.md).
