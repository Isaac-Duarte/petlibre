# Firmware feature board

This board tracks replacement-firmware integration, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Status records evidence from the research unit only; it is not a compatibility guarantee for another unit. For the installed artifact, bench state, evidence index and next task, start with [RESUME.md](RESUME.md).

This board covers what the SDK and hardware can do safely: peripherals, supervision, storage, transport, OTA and the feeding guarantees. Owner-visible behavior built on them (schedules, alerts, lock, light and sound policy, Home Assistant) and stock parity are on [FEATURE_BOARD.md](FEATURE_BOARD.md).

## Current status

- **Done:** FW01–FW15, FW20, FW24, FW31–FW33, FW35–FW41, several of them with recorded scope limits (see carried items).
- **In progress:** FW30: the motor driver (TC118AH) reads standby in the ROM loader and brake at firmware STOP; emitter OFF levels, transients and the GPIO-to-input mapping remain. FW21: first sync, jump classification and drift bound pass; restart across a long uptime remains. FW25: device options and Wi-Fi credentials pass; broker credentials and a second network remain. FW27: the integration suite runs smoke, standard and soak tiers; stall, sensor, jam, low-power and feeding-phase scenarios remain. FW34: BLE runs beside Wi-Fi on the feeder (link and smoke tier with an earlier speaker build); the `ble` firmware gives an offline feeder its Wi-Fi credentials over BLE, which it then joins (physical, Oct 23). The stock round trip is UART-free on hardware (FW40, FW41: `fw41-roundtrip-01`). FW42 (consumer installer) and FW43 (rollback) are ready.
- **Product platform (Oct 23):** one `feeder` firmware with an optional server, a feeder-hosted API, BLE pairing, offline time and native Home Assistant ([product architecture](docs/firmware/product-architecture.md)). FW44 (resource budget) and FW50 (device layer) are ready and need no motion; FW45–FW49 follow FW44.
- **Critical path to feeding:** FW30 → FW16 → FW17, which also leads stock parity ([FEATURE_BOARD.md](FEATURE_BOARD.md)).

## Using the board

Move cards through **Planned → Ready → In progress → Bench check → Done**. Ready requires completed dependencies. A Done card that leaves part of its finish line to another card says so in its status and in the carried-items table. The Evidence column records what acceptance rests on: physical bench observation, static analysis, or host simulation. Update the board when meaningful implementation or acceptance evidence changes. Keep one integration card in progress; independent software preparation can proceed in parallel.

Hardware acceptance belongs to an explicitly selected bench device and an operator-supervised procedure. No status here authorizes flashing, motion, or another stock round trip. Preserve raw evidence privately and record redacted outcomes. Host tests alone do not complete hardware acceptance.

Prioritize the UART development loop and build identity, stopped startup, LED diagnostics, then real flash coordination and Wi-Fi/OTA. Sensors and durable storage lead into supervised feeding; everyday features and fault/sustained testing follow. See [architecture](docs/firmware/README.md), [startup/indicator diagnostics](docs/firmware/startup-diagnostics.md), [development updater](docs/development/uart-development.md), and [recovery limits](docs/hardware/recovery.md).
